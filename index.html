MicCam 隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月1日
开发者：Pengwei Lv

MicCam 用设备摄像头录像，并用已连接的蓝牙麦克风或本机麦克风收音。视频保存在你的设备上。开发者不会接收你的视频、声音或位置。

收集和使用的信息

  • 相机。仅在你使用 MicCam 时调用摄像头，用来显示预览和录制画面。
  • 麦克风。用来录制已连接的蓝牙麦克风或本机麦克风的声音，并合成到视频里。
  • 位置。仅在你使用 MicCam 时获取。位置会写入视频，并在系统相册中显示拍摄地点。开发者不会收到这个位置。
  • 相册。只把你录好的视频添加到相册，不会读取相册里已有的照片或视频。

这些权限都可以拒绝。拒绝相机或麦克风后无法完成对应的录像。拒绝定位后，视频里不会有拍摄地点。拒绝添加到相册后，视频无法保存到相册。

不会做的事

  • 没有账号，不要求注册或登录。
  • 不把视频、声音、位置或通讯录上传到开发者的服务器。
  • 不使用广告，不追踪你在其他 App 或网站上的活动。
  • 不出售个人信息。

信息保存在哪里

录像和其中的位置信息保存在你的设备以及系统相册中。删除 MicCam 不会自动删除已经保存到相册的视频。要删除这些视频，请在相册中删除。

儿童

MicCam 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会明知地收集儿童的个人信息。

政策更新

如果以后的做法有变化，会更新本页，并修改上面的生效日期。

联系

MicCam Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026
Developer: Pengwei Lv

MicCam records video with your device camera and audio from a connected Bluetooth microphone or the built-in microphone. Recordings stay on your device. The developer does not receive your video, audio, or location.

Information we use

  • Camera. Used only while MicCam is open, to show the preview and record video.
  • Microphone. Used to record a connected Bluetooth microphone or the built-in microphone and add that audio to the video.
  • Location. Used only while MicCam is open. The location is written into the video and shown as the filming place in Photos. The developer does not receive it.
  • Photos. Used only to add the recordings you save. MicCam does not read existing photos or videos.

You can deny any of these permissions. Without camera or microphone access, recording cannot be completed. Without location access, videos will not include a filming location. Without permission to add to Photos, videos cannot be saved there.

What we do not do

  • MicCam has no account and does not ask you to register or sign in.
  • Video, audio, location, and contacts are not uploaded to the developer’s servers.
  • MicCam does not show ads and does not track your activity across other apps or websites.
  • MicCam does not sell personal information.

Where information is stored

Recordings and the location stored in them remain on your device and in Photos. Deleting MicCam does not delete videos already saved to Photos. Delete those videos in Photos.

Children

MicCam is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If these practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
